**Hero image placement — quick take:**

Default placement is **right of the headline on desktop, stacked below on mobile** — that's the standard SaaS pattern (Linear, Stripe, Vercel) because the eye lands on the headline first, then the image reinforces. Put it **above the fold** so it's the first thing visible without scrolling.

Two tradeoffs worth naming:
- **Right-of-headline (image as proof)**: best when the image *shows the product* (UI screenshot, product mock). Headline does the work; image just confirms.
- **Full-width background (image as mood)**: best when you're selling a feeling or category (devtools-for-creatives, founder-narrative). Risky — kills readability if not handled carefully.

If you don't know which yet, that's the real question to settle before placement. What does the hero image *show* — the product, or the vibe?
